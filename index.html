Racing is his passion – engineering his experience.

Wolfgang started racing with 8 years in a cart. Since that he was always in racing beside his career as technical engineer in industrial and environmental projects worldwide. Having successfully finished this part of his life he started with a small garage in Belgium in 2017, specialized on oldtimers and racing but not dedicated to that he is also offering full electronic diagnostic for nowadays cars.

His core experience is refurbishing parts – not replacing them. The whole equipment for metal works, surface treatment, electrical works and refurbishment including glassfibre and carbon works are available in his garage – and frequently in use for his customers. He likes the personal chat with his customers and is happy to explain all the steps needed to solve an actual problem on a car, as well as guiding the owner to get it customized for comfort or motorsport issues.

As passionate racer he is also happy to assist race drivers during a complete racing session – ask him.

Finally in 2017 Wolfgang's dream of life became reality – his own garage.

Simply starting with a service truck for race assistance the garage got equipment and with a growing number of customers his own place to work there every day.
